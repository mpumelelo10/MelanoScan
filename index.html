Learn about skin cancer

🎗️ Types of skin cancer

Melanoma, BCC, SCC and more

Melanoma – Most serious. Often a new or changing mole.

Basal Cell Carcinoma – Most common. Pearly or waxy bumps.

Squamous Cell Carcinoma – Scaly red patches or open sores.

🔍 ABCDE warning signs

How to check moles yourself

Asymmetry · Border · Colour · Diameter · Evolving

On darker skin, check palms, soles, nails and scalp carefully.

☀️ Prevention & sun safety

SPF, shade, peak hours

• Avoid peak UV (10:00–16:00)

• SPF 30+ every day, reapply every 2 hours

• Protective clothing, hats, sunglasses

• Never use tanning beds

🖤 Skin of colour awareness

Why early detection matters for African skin

Skin cancer affects all tones. On darker skin it is often found later. Melanoma may appear on palms, soles or under nails.

🏥 When to see a doctor

Don’t wait if you notice these signs

See a doctor if a spot is new and growing, changes colour/shape, bleeds, itches, or doesn’t heal.
